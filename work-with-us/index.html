Work with Tressed Out

Tressed Out tells the stories behind the hair industry for people who love hair: hairdressers, beauty lovers, history buffs and anyone who grew up with a can of hairspray.

Who watches

Our audience is international, with the USA our biggest market. Viewers include working hairdressers, salon owners, beauty enthusiasts and people who love the history and nostalgia behind the trends.

Where we publish

  • YouTube: a new long-form story every two weeks.
  • Facebook and TikTok: short reels from every story.
  • tressedout.com: the full written story behind each video, with sources.

Ways to work together

  • Sponsored segments in a video.
  • Product and brand stories that fit the channel.
  • Media, interviews and expert comment on hair industry history.

We only work with brands that fit what our audience trusts us for. Sponsored content is always labeled.
