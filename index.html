About Tressed Out

There's more to hair than meets the eye. Tressed Out covers the fascinating, shocking and surprising stories behind the hair industry: the inventions, the scandals, the icons and the stylists nobody remembers.

Every story is told by a hairdresser who has spent years behind the chair, from the big perms of the 80s to today.
